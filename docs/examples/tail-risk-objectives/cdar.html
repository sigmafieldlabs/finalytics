Method

How the weights were found

How the weights were found

MethodMinimum CDaR: the smallest average of the worst drawdowns, at the confidence given, of the historical returns added up period by period.
DataUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
EstimatesHistorical mean expected returns and the Ledoit–Wolf covariance, annualised by 12.
LimitsAt most 50% in any asset.
SolverInterior point, optimal after 24 iterations.
