Method

How the weights were found

How the weights were found

MethodMaximum Sharpe: the largest expected return in excess of the risk-free rate per unit of volatility, solved as a quadratic programme after the Charnes–Cooper transformation.
DataUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
EstimatesHistorical mean expected returns and the Ledoit–Wolf covariance, annualised by 12.
Limits0% to 40% in each asset; Gold: 0% to 10%; Equity (US Equity, Intl Equity): 30% to 60% together.
SolverInterior point, optimal after 10 iterations.
