Method

How the weights were found

How the weights were found

MethodMaximum Sharpe: the largest expected return in excess of the risk-free rate per unit of volatility, solved as a quadratic programme after the Charnes–Cooper transformation.
DataBND, EXSA, GLD, VNQ and VTI; USD cash as the risk-free asset; EUR cash as cash. Daily returns, Nov 2023 to Oct 2026.
EstimatesHistorical mean expected returns and the Ledoit–Wolf covariance, annualised by 252.
LimitsAt most 15% turnover from the current weights; The cash held now kept at its weight.
SolverInterior point, optimal after 10 iterations.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0 from the assets' returns; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest 6cea2786e85e.
