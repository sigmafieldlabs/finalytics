Method

How the backtest was run

How the allocation was tested

AssetsUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset, held as cash. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
ValidationA holdout: weights fitted to the history to Oct 2016 and scored on Nov 2016 to Oct 2026, returns they had not seen.
EstimatesThe Ledoit–Wolf covariance and historical mean returns, annualised by 12.
LimitsAt most 40% in any asset; fully invested.
RebalancingQuarterly, to the targets; between rebalances the weights drift with prices.
Trading costs10 bp of the value traded, paid from the next period's return.
In sampleThe same weights over the history they were fitted to, Nov 1996 to Oct 2016.
RatiosSharpe and Sortino ratios against the T-Bills return over each period scored (2.5% a year over the test); value at risk and expected shortfall from history, at 95% over one month.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0 from the assets' returns; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest 3da1af4b14e3.
