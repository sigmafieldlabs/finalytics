Method

How the backtest was run

How the allocation was tested

AssetsUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset, held as cash. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
ValidationFixed weights, not fitted to these returns, scored on Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
RebalancingYearly, to the targets; between rebalances the weights drift with prices.
Trading costsNone.
RatiosSharpe and Sortino ratios against the T-Bills return over each period scored (2.3% a year over the test); value at risk and expected shortfall from history, at 95% over one month.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0 from the assets' returns; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest c9acf1f95891.
