How the allocation was tested

ValidationFixed weights, not fitted to these returns, scored on Nov 2023 to Oct 2026.
RebalancingMonthly, to the targets; between rebalances the weights drift with prices.
Trading costs10 bp of the value traded, paid from the next period's return.
