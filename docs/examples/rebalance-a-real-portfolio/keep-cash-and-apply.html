Method

How the trades were found

How the trades were found

TargetsMinimum variance, 5% to 40% in each asset, from an optimisation.
AccountsBrokerage account and Retirement account, rebalanced together. A holding above its target is sold from the accounts that hold it, in proportion to their holdings; one below it is bought in proportion to the value each account has free. Trades do not move cash between accounts.
Prices30 Oct 2026; other currencies at the exchange rates of that day.
TradesWhole units, each rounded towards zero. Purchases leave room for the fees, so the trades fit the cash each account has.
CashEUR 3,132 and USD 27,015 kept as they are, as asked: the targets are shares of the rest of the value, so the trades neither invest it nor add to it.
LotsEach account's own lot method (first in, first out unless the account says otherwise).
FeesNone.
TaxNot estimated: no tax rates were given.
EstimatesVolatility and expected return from the holdings' daily returns, Nov 2023 to Oct 2026, annualised: the Ledoit–Wolf covariance and historical means. Cash is riskless and earns nothing here.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0 from the portfolio's records and prices; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest 676ae8a79063.
