Method

How the comparison was run

How the methods were tested

AssetsUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset, held as cash. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
ValidationWalk-forward. At each rebalance from Nov 2001, each method is fitted to the previous 60 months of returns and its weights are held until the next. Every return scored is one the method had not seen when it chose its weights.
EstimatesThe Ledoit–Wolf covariance and historical mean returns, annualised by 12.
LimitsAt most 40% in any asset; fully invested.
RebalancingQuarterly, to the targets; between rebalances the weights drift with prices.
Trading costs10 bp of the value traded, paid from the next period's return.
In sampleThe same methods fitted once to all the history and held over the same periods, for comparison.
RatiosSharpe and Sortino ratios against the T-Bills return over each period scored (1.8% a year over the test); value at risk and expected shortfall from history, at 95% over one month.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0-rc.7 from the assets' returns; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest 170629937beb.
