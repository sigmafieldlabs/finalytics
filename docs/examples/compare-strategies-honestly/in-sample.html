Method

How the comparison was run

How the methods were tested

AssetsUS Equity, Intl Equity, Bonds, Gold and Real Estate; T-Bills as the risk-free asset, held as cash. Monthly returns, Nov 1996 to Oct 2026.
ValidationIn sample: weights fitted once to the whole history and scored on it, with knowledge of the very returns they are scored on.
EstimatesThe Ledoit–Wolf covariance and historical mean returns, annualised by 12.
LimitsAt most 40% in any asset; fully invested.
RebalancingQuarterly, to the targets; between rebalances the weights drift with prices.
Trading costsNone.
RatiosSharpe and Sortino ratios against the T-Bills return over each period scored (2.3% a year over the test); value at risk and expected shortfall from history, at 95% over one month.
Every figure is computed by Finalytics 1.0.0-rc.7 from the assets' returns; the same inputs give the same report, identified by the input digest c8b35a9dfd77.
